How it works

  1. Find the businesses that need a website

    Type a trade and a town. Flash Builder lists every match from Google, checks each website, and sorts out the ones with no site, only a Facebook or Instagram page, a broken site, or an outdated one.

  2. Build their demo site in seconds

    One click makes a full website with their name, phone number, address and opening hours, in a style that suits their trade. Add pages for services, prices or their story, and change colors, photos and video.

  3. Send the pitch

    Put the demo online at its own private link and send a short, personal email with it. Flash Builder writes the draft, keeps you inside the email rules, and remembers who you've contacted.

What you need

Flash Builder runs on your own computer, so your leads stay with you. You connect your own accounts once, in about 30 minutes, and a setup check tells you what's still missing.

  • A Windows, Mac or Linux computer with Node.js, which is free.
  • A Google Cloud key for searching. Google's free monthly allowance covers about 1,000 searches.
  • Gmail, if you want to send emails from the app. Optional.
  • A free Netlify account, if you want to put demos online. Optional.
  • A free Pexels or Pixabay key for better stock photos and video. Optional.
