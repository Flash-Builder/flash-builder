    Customize demo

    Colors

    The main color is adjusted darker if needed so button text stays readable.

    Color one part of the page

    Give the whole page, the header, the top section or any other part its own background. Text color is picked automatically so it stays readable, unless you set it.

      Pages

      The home page is always there. Add more pages and give each one its own title. Every page gets the same look, colors and effects, with a layout made for its type. They appear in the menu in this order.

        Top section

        MP4 or WebM, up to 30 MB. A 10 to 20 second clip works best. It plays muted on a loop, and visitors who turn off motion see a still photo instead.

        Photos

        The business's own photos (replace the stock ones, up to 6)

          Only use photos the business sent you or agreed you can use.

          Reviews

          Only add reviews the business gave you, and tick consent for each. Invented reviews presented as real are illegal in the US. Reviews without consent are left off the page.

            Setup check

            Flash Builder tries each account in your .env file once. It doesn't use your Google allowance or send any email. After changing .env, restart the app and check again.

              Published demos

              Tick the demos to put online or update, then publish. Demos you don't tick stay online exactly as they are. Each demo has its own link; the site's front page doesn't list them, so only people you send a link to can find theirs.

                Email

                  Edit anything you like. Keep the last two lines: US law needs your postal address and the opt-out in every email.
